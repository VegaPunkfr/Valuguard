
## Usage Notes

- **One page means one page.** If you can't fit it, your thinking isn't clear enough.
- **No appendices.** If the decision-maker needs more data, link to it but don't attach it.
- **Options must include "do nothing."** The status quo is always an option. Make its cost explicit.
- **The key assumption is the most important line.** Get it wrong and everything downstream fails. Get it right and the rest is execution.
- **Kill metrics are mandatory.** Every initiative must have a defined failure threshold. Without it, zombie strategies consume resources indefinitely.
