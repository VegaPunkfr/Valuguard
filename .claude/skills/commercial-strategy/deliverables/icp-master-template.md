## 8. ICP Validity Check

Review quarterly. Answer honestly.

- [ ] Are we still finding enough prospects matching this ICP? (Volume)
- [ ] Are they still converting at acceptable rates? (Quality)
- [ ] Is the pain point we target still their top priority? (Relevance)
- [ ] Can we reach them through our available channels? (Accessibility)
- [ ] Is the LTV from this ICP worth the CAC? (Economics)

**If 2+ answers are NO → trigger kill criteria review per revision-protocol.md**
