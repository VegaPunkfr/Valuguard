# Landing Page Strategy Template — Credibility Architecture

## Purpose

Design landing pages that convert through credibility, not hype. Every section earns the right to ask for the next action.

  Anti-example: "AI-Powered Decision Intelligence Platform"

## Page Architecture

### Section 1: Hero (Above the Fold)
**Job**: Stop the scroll. Establish relevance in 3 seconds.

- [ ] Headline (core message)
- [ ] Subheadline (mechanism or objection removal)
- [ ] Primary CTA
- [ ] Visual: [PLACEHOLDER — what does the visitor see? Screenshot, illustration, data visualization?]
  - Guidance: Show the OUTPUT (what they get), not the INPUT (what they do)
- [ ] Social proof micro-element: [PLACEHOLDER — one credibility signal. "Analyzed 2,847 companies" or "Average finding: 47k EUR"]

**What this section earns**: Permission to keep scrolling.

---

### Section 2: Problem Validation
**Job**: Make the visitor feel understood. Prove you know their world.

- [ ] Problem statement: [PLACEHOLDER — describe their pain in their language, not yours]
- [ ] Quantified impact: [PLACEHOLDER — how much is this problem costing them? Use industry data.]
- [ ] Recognition moment: [PLACEHOLDER — the specific detail that makes them think "they get it"]
  - Guidance: Use language from actual prospect conversations or industry reports
  - Example: "Your SaaS spend grew 34% last year. Your headcount grew 12%. Nobody asked why."

**What this section earns**: Trust that you understand the problem.

---

### Section 3: Solution (How It Works)
**Job**: Explain the mechanism. Remove "how does this work?" anxiety.

- [ ] Step 1: [PLACEHOLDER — what does the user do first?]
- [ ] Step 2: [PLACEHOLDER — what happens next?]
- [ ] Step 3: [PLACEHOLDER — what do they get?]
  - Guidance: Max 3 steps. Each step should feel easy and low-risk.
  - Guidance: Emphasize what's different about your approach (e.g., "zero system access")
- [ ] CTA repeat: [Same primary CTA]

**What this section earns**: Confidence that this is achievable and safe.

---

### Section 4: Proof
**Job**: Back up every claim with evidence. This is where credibility is built or destroyed.

Choose proof types appropriate to stage:

**Pre-revenue (Ghost Tax current stage)**:
- [ ] Methodology credibility: [How does the analysis work? What data sources?]
- [ ] Sample output: [Redacted sample of the actual deliverable]
- [ ] Industry data: [Third-party statistics that validate the problem]
- [ ] Founder credibility: [Relevant expertise, not generic bio]

**Post-revenue (upgrade to these as you earn them)**:
- [ ] Case study: [Specific company, specific result, specific timeline]
- [ ] Testimonial: [Named person, real title, real company, specific quote]
- [ ] Aggregate proof: ["Average finding per report: 47k EUR across N companies"]
- [ ] Logo bar: [Recognizable customer logos with permission]

**Proof quality rules**:
- Specific > generic ("47,000 EUR" > "significant savings")
- Named > anonymous ("Maria K., CFO at Mittelstand GmbH" > "a CFO")
- Recent > old (this quarter > last year)
- Verified > claimed (link to source > "studies show")

**What this section earns**: Belief that results are real and repeatable.

## Page Rules

### Do:
- Lead with the visitor's problem, not your solution
- Use numbers over adjectives
- Show the deliverable (what they get)
- Make the CTA describe what happens next
- Test one variable at a time

### Do Not:
- Use "AI-powered" or "cutting-edge" or "revolutionary" in any headline
- Put multiple CTAs competing for attention in the same viewport
- Use stock photos of people in suits looking at screens
- Write "no obligation" or "no commitment" (signals low confidence)
- Hide the price (creates distrust)

### Mobile Requirements
- [ ] Hero CTA visible without scrolling on mobile
- [ ] All text readable at 16px minimum
- [ ] Tap targets minimum 44x44px
- [ ] Page loads in <3 seconds on 3G
